sequence:

Open IMR with an order loaded. Turn the volume down. Ctrl+Windows+Enter to start Narrator, click into the part number table, press the down arrow a few times, Ctrl+Windows+Enter to stop.

Open IMR, load an order.

Paste the handoff prompt into Copilot.

Run 01-probe.ps1 in PowerShell.

Paste its output to Copilot.

Copilot reads the output with you and tells you the three config values, mainly which semicolon field holds the part number.

Open 02-part-search.ps1 in Notepad, put those three values in the CONFIG block, save.

Paste 02-part-search.ps1 into PowerShell. Search box appears. Test it.
